# Scoring Model Notes

This document tracks the public methodology for translating source evidence into role-risk scores.

## Candidate dimensions

| Dimension | Meaning | Example evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Task exposure | Share of work tasks AI can assist or automate | task studies, occupational task databases |
| Adoption speed | How quickly tools are entering the workflow | surveys, enterprise reports, job postings |
| Capability fit | How well current AI handles the role's tasks | benchmarks, field studies, expert review |
| Physical constraint | Whether work requires physical presence or dexterity | occupation descriptions, robotics adoption |
| Trust constraint | Whether human accountability is legally or socially required | regulation, licensing, safety norms |
| Economic pressure | Incentive to substitute or redesign labor | wages, margins, outsourcing patterns |
| Observed displacement | Evidence of layoffs, hiring slowdown, or restructuring | company reports, layoff trackers, news |
| Uplift without displacement | Evidence AI increases productivity while retaining workers | field experiments, case studies |

## Output guidance

Scores should be rounded and presented as an index. Avoid false precision.

## Review guidance

Before publishing a new score or change:

1. Identify the source evidence.
2. Separate observed data from interpretation.
3. Note confidence and limitations.
4. Record the change in a public or private changelog as appropriate.
