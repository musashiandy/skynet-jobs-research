## S22 — The Employment Situation: October 2026

- Publisher: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics.
- Published: November 6, 2026, 8:30 a.m. ET.
- URL: [archived release](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_11062026.htm).
- Evidence: official household and establishment surveys for October; supplies the current aggregate and healthcare employment backdrop.
- Limits: estimates are revisable; these surveys do not identify AI's causal contribution, documentation-role outcomes, or staffing effects at an individual employer. Household unemployment and establishment payrolls have different survey bases.
- Used in: full drafts 01 and 06. Not present in the November 5 briefs.

## S23 — October Layoffs at Three-Year Low; Seasonal Hiring Plans Up 26%

- Publisher: Challenger, Gray & Christmas, Inc.
- Published: November 5, 2026.
- URL: [original report page](https://www.challengergray.com/blog/october-layoffs-at-three-year-low-seasonal-hiring-plans-up-26/).
- Evidence: announced cuts, employer-cited reasons, and hiring plans through October. Useful for distinguishing announcements from net employment and checking sector classifications.
- Limits: planned changes and stated reasons are not completed outcomes or independently verified AI replacement. Technology-sector hiring includes Amazon seasonal fulfillment roles; the label alone cannot identify developer recruitment.
- Used in: full drafts 01 and 02. The original November 5 briefs did not use this report.

## S24 — Who Is AI Replacing? The Impact of Generative AI on Online Freelancing Platforms

- Authors: Ozge Demirci, Jonas Hannane, Xinrong Zhu.
- Publisher: *Management Science*, INFORMS; 71(10):8097–8108.
- Published online: January 24, 2025; October 2025 issue.
- URL: [publisher abstract and bibliographic record](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/abs/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05420); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1287/mnsc.2024.05420).
- Evidence: peer-reviewed analysis of platform demand during the early post-ChatGPT period, distinguishing posting volume and the characteristics of remaining work.
- Limits: publisher abstract reviewed; early historical observations are not a 2026 earnings series, and platform vacancies do not directly measure individual income. No numerical effect estimate is repeated in the draft.
- Used in: full draft 09, replacing S18 as supporting evidence.
