# Microsoft Research: Working with AI

## Source

- Title: Working with AI: Measuring the Occupational Implications of Generative AI
- Publisher: Microsoft Research
- URL: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/working-with-ai-measuring-the-occupational-implications-of-generative-ai/
- Date accessed: 2026-07-10

## What this supports

- Occupation-level and task-level generative AI applicability framing
- Interpretation of where AI may be useful inside work activities
- Evidence that AI impact should be analyzed below the job-title level

## Related Skynet Jobs analysis

- Task compression is the first labor shock
- The new career moat is verification
- The spreadsheet economy is an AI exposure zone

## Limitations

Applicability is not displacement. This source should support task-exposure interpretation, not deterministic job-loss claims.
