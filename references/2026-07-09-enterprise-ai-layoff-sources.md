## Microsoft 2026 layoffs

Primary/official source:

- Title: The latest in our company transformation
- Publisher: Microsoft
- URL: https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/07/06/the-latest-in-our-company-transformation/
- Supports: Microsoft framed recent cuts inside a broader company transformation and acknowledged AI changing how work is done.
- Caveat: Microsoft also stated that eliminated jobs were not being directly replaced by AI, so this should be labeled AI-linked restructuring rather than direct AI replacement.

- Supports: approximate cut size, affected units including sales and Xbox, and the tension between AI transformation framing and direct replacement denial.
