# OECD AI and Work

## Source

- Title: Artificial intelligence and work
- Publisher: OECD
- URL: https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/sub-issues/ai-and-work.html
- Date accessed: 2026-07-10

## What this supports

- Task-level exposure framing
- Policy-oriented interpretation of AI in labor markets
- Distinction between exposure, adoption, productivity, and employment outcomes
- Caution around interpreting AI capability as direct displacement

## Related Skynet Jobs analysis

- Task compression is the first labor shock
- Why AI may hollow out the middle of knowledge work before it replaces experts
- The spreadsheet economy is an AI exposure zone
- AI will create work too — but not always where workers expect it

## Limitations

OECD materials are useful for policy and exposure framing. They should not be treated as proof that a specific occupation will gain or lose a specific number of jobs.
