## Access and evidence gaps

- S09: manuscript content retrieved through indexed source text; direct opening intermittently challenged the browser. Use the DOI and publisher record to recheck final wording.
- S10, S11, S18: abstract-level review only. The briefs stay within abstract-supported claims. Full-text methods and later revisions remain part of article reporting.
- S12 and S19: first-party releases provide useful leads but incomplete underlying sampling/adjustment details. Attribute findings and seek the fuller methods.
- S17: exact marketplace comparison endpoints are absent from the release. Retain “year over year, as reported in July” until verified.
- S04: the living dashboard is not a frozen July dataset; the text mentions a July release. Capture a dated data version before making a chart or latest-endpoint claim.
- A search lead to an Atlanta Fed October PDF returned 404 and was excluded. Search snippets, inaccessible leads, secondary headlines, and publication dates alone were not accepted as numerical evidence.
- The November 5 briefs exclude later events. The November 7 full drafts add the November 6 employment release as S22; the refresh supplement records that extension explicitly.

## Reuse and publication boundaries

Only short original summaries and links are stored. No source reports, proprietary data, copied charts, invented interviews, or live-site changes are included. Most proposed visuals are conceptual; numerical graphics still require verified source data and permissions review where applicable.

Editorial review should record the final article version, review date, reviewer, source versions, verified numerical claims, and any unresolved limitations. Publication and tracker changes are separate actions. See [the series index](README.md) and [repository methodology](../../METHODOLOGY.md).
