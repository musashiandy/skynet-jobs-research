## Evidence and editorial approach

The [original source register](../../references/2026-11-05-ai-market-series-sources.md) records 21 original research or first-party sources, bibliographic details, observation windows, and limitations. The [November 7 supplement](../../references/2026-11-07-ai-market-series-draft-refresh.md) adds S22–S24 and documents a source replacement in full article 09. Each brief and draft links directly to its key sources. Sources include government economists, university researchers, peer-reviewed studies, company filings, trade-association statistics, and commercial research; their authority and incentives differ.

The [claim-check ledger](claim-check-ledger.md) identifies the numbers and interpretations most likely to be distorted during writing. A publication date is not the date of the measured event. For example, September robotics releases describe 2025 activity; a March hiring study ends in November 2025. The series is current research selected as of the cutoff, not a claim that every underlying dataset reaches November.

Observed patterns, survey responses, quasi-experimental estimates, company expectations, scenarios, and our own interpretation must remain distinguishable. A role-risk score is not a probability of losing a job. None of these briefs changes scores, layoff totals, or website datasets.
