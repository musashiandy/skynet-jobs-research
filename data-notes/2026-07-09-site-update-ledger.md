## Analysis article added

### Meta layoffs and the hidden labor logic of AI efficiency

Public URL planned:

- https://jobs.skynetproxy.com/analysis/meta-layoffs-ai-efficiency

Research interpretation:

- The article treats Meta's 2026 workforce changes as an AI-linked restructuring signal, not as proof that AI directly replaced each affected worker.
- The central claim is that AI can change enterprise economics before it cleanly replaces workers: capital allocation, org design, productivity expectations, management layers, and labor carrying costs.
- The article includes a caveat that Meta's own internal comments reportedly suggested agent progress had been slower than expected, meaning labor changes can arrive before the AI payoff is fully proven.

Related tracker interpretation:

- Category: Enterprise Signals
- Suggested evidence label: AI-linked restructuring
- Suggested AI link score: 85
