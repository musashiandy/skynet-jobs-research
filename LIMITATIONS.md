# Limitations

Skynet Jobs is an interpretive research and visualization project, not an official labor-market forecast.

## Important caveats

- AI exposure is not the same as job loss.
- Productivity gain is not the same as wage gain or employment growth.
- Occupation-level risk can hide large variation between tasks, employers, regions, and seniority levels.
- Public layoff attribution can be incomplete or shaped by company messaging.
- Different sources define AI adoption differently.
- Historical automation studies may not fully capture generative AI or agentic AI.
- Survey forecasts should be separated from observed outcomes.

## Confidence

Public metrics should include confidence notes where possible. A precise-looking number can still be uncertain.

## Update lag

Official statistics can lag real-world changes. Fast-moving AI adoption signals may come from surveys, company disclosures, job postings, or research studies before official datasets reflect them.

## Responsible framing

The site should avoid deterministic claims about specific people or careers. It should help readers understand pressure, exposure, and adaptation pathways.
